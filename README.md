# NHS Bed Pressure Early Warning Dashboard (Winter 2023–24)

## Executive Summary

This Power BI project analyses NHS bed capacity, occupancy rates, and healthcare pressure across NHS trusts and regions during Winter 2023–24.

The dashboard was developed to provide insights into bed utilisation, regional performance, and operational pressure, supporting data-driven healthcare decision-making.

---

## Project Objectives

- Monitor NHS bed occupancy and capacity.
- Identify regions experiencing high healthcare pressure.
- Analyse trust-level performance.
- Track bed utilisation trends over time.
- Support operational planning through data visualisation.

## Key Insights

- Occupancy rates remained above 92% throughout Winter 2023–24.
- Open bed capacity increased significantly from November to December.
- East of England maintained the highest available bed capacity.
- Several NHS trusts operated above 95% occupancy.
- Bed pressure remained consistently high across the reporting period.
